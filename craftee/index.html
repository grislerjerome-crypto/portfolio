Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build my website?

Preview page within 1 to 3 days. For a complete, fully functioning store, launch takes up to 2 weeks, depending on catalog size and custom features.

Can you upload my products for me?

Yes . Product setup, photos, variants, sizes and colors are part of every package.

Can I manage the products myself?

Not needed . Craftee Sites manages your catalog for you as part of the monthly care plan. Just send what you want changed and we'll update it. You also get a live overview to watch your store's performance.

Can customers pay directly through my website?

Yes. Your customers can securely make payments directly on your website at checkout using credit or debit card, Visa, and more.

Can I manage inventory?

We manage stock, sizes, colors, low stock and out of stock for you as part of care. And you can see it anytime in your live store overview.

Can I offer discount codes?

Yes . Coupon codes, percentage/fixed discounts, free shipping and bundles.

Can I connect my domain?

Yes . We connect your existing domain at launch.

Is the website mobile-friendly?

Fully responsive and mobile-optimized, built mobile first.

Can you integrate shipping?

Yes . Shipping zones, rates and tracking are configured for you.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes . We can rebuild or restyle an existing store on a modern stack.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes . Every plan includes $19.99/mo care; Premium adds priority support.

Can I request custom features?

Yes. Custom features and integrations are available on Starter and Premium.
